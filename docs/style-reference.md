# Style reference - Farland Story 2

Approved voice for every translator (pilot: chapters 0-1, b001; the owner pre-approved the run, D6). Who is who
and the reveals: `docs/synopsis.md`. Names: `glossary.md`.

## Pages

Each ` || ` page is one key press in the game. Pages split mid-sentence in the Japanese stay split in the English
at a natural point ("It wasn't until I left the castle that I || saw how utterly empty || war really is."). A
lone name line (リアド・・・) stays its own page ("Riad...").
